Priority: FSS — You Pay Them to Advertise Here

🏪 Floral Supply Syndicate — FSS (fss.com)

Facu pays FSS to advertise Floropolis. Here's what their platform actually is — and what it isn't. Note: FSS ≠ FSN. FSN (Flower Shop Network, flowershopnetwork.com) is a florist-to-florist order relay — completely different. FSS is hardgoods.
📋

What it is: A wholesale hard goods supplier, founded 1939. Sells ribbon, vases, foam, packaging, baskets, seasonal décor. NOT fresh flowers. The florists browsing FSS are buying supplies — your ad shows up in a "supplies shopping" context, not a "sourcing flowers" context. These are still your buyers, but they're mid-task.

🗂

Catalog UX: Visitors can browse categories without logging in, but all pricing hidden behind login. Early-2000s interface — basic dropdowns, JavaScript galleries, zero visual search, no filtering beyond category. 21 physical US showrooms. Ships nationally. Strength is professional reorders, not discovery.

❌

No design tools whatsoever. No arrangement planner, no inspiration board, no visual search, no AI features, no integration with Curate or any design software.

🔑

Key insight — FSS data lives inside Curate: Curate (the leading event florist proposal software, $100-275/mo, thousands of users) already pulls the FSS hard goods catalog AND the Mayesh Wholesale cut-flower catalog directly into florists' proposals. Florists drag Mayesh photos right into their client proposals. FSS data reaches florists through Curate even though FSS's own site is terrible. Floropolis should get its catalog into Curate the same way Mayesh has — this is a distribution play, not a product build.

Floropolis Opportunity

FSS has the florist audience but zero fresh flower catalog, zero design tooling. Keep advertising there — but the bigger move is getting into Curate. Every Curate user (thousands of event florists) could source from Floropolis without leaving their workflow. That's bigger than ads.
